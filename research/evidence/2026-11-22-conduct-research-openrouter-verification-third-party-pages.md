Each section says who is speaking and whether a maintainer or vendor confirmed it.

## Part 1. Other gateways' documentation

### VT1. Vercel AI Gateway's chat route copies OpenRouter's reasoning fields (Q2, Q3, Q4)

- Verdict: confirmed.
- Page: https://vercel.com/docs/ai-gateway/sdks-and-apis/openai-chat-completions/reasoning (VT-s1). The Markdown form of the page has `last_updated: 2026-09-08`. Speaker: Vercel, in its own product documentation.
- `message.reasoning` and `delta.reasoning`: "When the provider returns reasoning content, AI Gateway includes it in the response", with the example `"reasoning": "Let me think about this carefully. The question asks about..."` inside `message`, and "Reasoning content is streamed incrementally in the `delta.reasoning` field".
- `reasoning_details`: "When `reasoning.enabled` is `true` (or when `reasoning.exclude` is not set), responses include a `reasoning_details` array alongside the standard `reasoning` text field. This structured field captures cryptographic signatures, encrypted content, and other verification data that providers include with their reasoning output."
- The three entry types, quoted: "`'reasoning.text'`: Contains the actual reasoning content as plain text in the `text` field. May include a `signature` field (Anthropic models) for cryptographic verification." "`'reasoning.encrypted'`: Contains encrypted or redacted reasoning content in the `data` field. Used by OpenAI models when reasoning is protected, or by Anthropic models when thinking is redacted. Preserves the encrypted payload for verification purposes." "`'reasoning.summary'`: Contains a condensed version of the reasoning process in the `summary` field."
- The `format` tags, quoted: "`format`: Provider format identifier, such as `'openai-responses-v1'`, `'anthropic-claude-v1'`, `'google-gemini-v1'`, `'xai-responses-v1'`, or `'unknown'`". Also "`id` (optional)" and "`index` (optional): Position in the reasoning sequence (for responses with multiple reasoning blocks)".
- The request object, quoted from the parameter table: "`enabled` | boolean | Request reasoning on or off.", "`effort` | string | The request schema accepts `none`, `minimal`, `low`, `medium`, `high`, `xhigh`, or `max`.", "`max_tokens` | number | Request a thinking-token budget. Can't be combined with `effort`.", "`exclude` | boolean | When `true`, omit reasoning content from the response. It doesn't turn off internal reasoning." And: "The `reasoning` object is a Gateway extension to the OpenAI Chat Completions API." and "the standard `reasoning_effort` field is an alias for `reasoning.effort`. If both are present, the nested value takes precedence."
- Does it state a replay rule? The page's whole statement about later turns is this: "The AI Gateway preserves reasoning details from models across interactions, normalizing the different formats used by OpenAI, Anthropic, and other providers into a consistent structure. This allows you to switch between models without rewriting your conversation management logic." and "This is particularly useful during tool calling workflows where the model needs to resume its thought process after receiving tool results." Every JSON example on the page is a response or a first request. A search of the page's Markdown for "replay", "unmodified", "unchanged", "verbatim", "send back", and "pass back" returned zero matches. So the first wave's reading holds: Vercel says what the array is for and leaves the send-back rule to the reader.

### VT2. LLM Gateway documents the replay rules OpenRouter keeps closed (Q31)

- Verdict: confirmed.
- The product exists. Home page (VT-s3): "LLM Gateway is an open-source AI gateway. Route requests through one OpenAI-compatible API and key". "Open source (AGPLv3) and self-hostable." Endpoint `https://api.llmgateway.io/v1`. Its own comparison list names OpenRouter: "How LLM Gateway compares to OpenRouter, LiteLLM, Portkey, the Vercel AI Gateway, and GitHub Copilot."
- Who runs it. About page (VT-s4): "LLM Gateway is operated by Polar Lights LLC, 16192 Coastal Highway, Lewes, DE 19958, United States, and developed in the open by a distributed team and community of contributors on GitHub. The full source is licensed under AGPLv3, with commercial enterprise licensing available for the features in the `ee/` directory." The public repository `theopenco/llmgateway` answers a `git ls-remote` today with HEAD `fd7e236c3faf86d31d9252e8f16a3fa49cb1a8aa` (VT-s5). Its own scale claim: "The platform has routed 1T+ tokens across 80M+ requests". That figure is the operator's own.
- Page: https://docs.llmgateway.io/features/reasoning (VT-s2). The page is undated. Speaker: the operator, in its own documentation.
- Same field and entry shape on chat completions: "The Chat Completions format has no reasoning items, so the gateway carries the same payloads on the assistant message as `reasoning_details`", with the entry `{"type": "reasoning.encrypted", "data": "gAAAAABqfkEO3uEFtXeGSNE9...", "id": "rs_057d93d1ba22357e01...", "format": "openai-responses-v1", "index": 0}`.
- Send-back rule: "To preserve the reasoning, append the assistant message back into `messages` exactly as you received it (with `reasoning_details` intact) before the matching `tool` result."
- Strips entries from other upstreams: "The gateway converts supported `reasoning_details` formats to native provider fields and strips these entries from other upstream requests."
- `block_index` and position: "Replay the assistant turn unchanged, including each entry's `block_index`. The gateway replays thinking only in the position where Claude produced it."
- Drops thinking blocks when the provider changes: "Signatures are scoped to the provider that issued them: when a conversation moves to another provider, the gateway drops that provider's thinking blocks instead of failing the request."
- `google_part` and signed boundaries: "Gemini text turns carry signatures in `reasoning_details` entries with `type: "reasoning.text"` and `format: "google-gemini-v1"`. Replay the entire assistant message, including these entries. Preserve each entry's `google_part` metadata: it lets the gateway restore the original signed text boundaries. Streaming clients must collect `delta.reasoning_details`, including entries arriving with empty text."
- Notes for the lead. (1) `block_index` and `google_part` appear in prose only. The page's single JSON entry shows `type`, `data`, `id`, `format`, `index`, so where these two members sit inside an entry is unshown. (2) The page names a third metadata member the first wave did not list: "When the gateway repairs JSON, clients receive the repaired answer. The entries also carry `google_response` metadata so replay can restore the original signed text. Editing the returned answer prevents that restoration." (3) The page also says "Only replay signatures with the original content and the model/provider that issued them." and, for errors, "A modified, truncated, or foreign payload is rejected upstream and the error is forwarded unchanged" with `"code": "invalid_encrypted_content"`. The Claude case drops quietly and the OpenAI encrypted case forwards the upstream error, so the two behaviors differ by format.

### VT5. Open Responses is a governed neutral specification for the Responses shape (Q53 to Q56)

- Verdict: confirmed with a correction (minor).
- Home: https://www.openresponses.org/ (VT-s18). "Open Responses is an open-source specification and ecosystem for building multi-provider, interoperable LLM interfaces based on the OpenAI Responses API." Specification: https://www.openresponses.org/specification (VT-s19). Repository: `openresponses/openresponses`, cloned today at commit `dce9ddb91921427c2ba06901c7dfaf0cc7056799`, commit date 2026-10-29 (VT-s21). This is the same commit the first wave read.
- Governance page: https://www.openresponses.org/governance (VT-s20), headed "Technical Charter (the “Charter”) for Open Responses a Series of LF Projects, LLC" and "Adopted October 21, 2026". "The TSC Chair, or any other TSC member so designated by the TSC, will serve as the primary communication contact between the Project and Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF), a directed fund of The Linux Foundation." "This charter may be amended by a two-thirds vote of the entire TSC and is subject to approval by LF Projects." "Linux Foundation project" is a fair short form of those two phrases.
- Maintainer from OpenRouter: `MAINTAINERS.md` at that commit says "The Maintainers are the initial voting members of the Technical Steering Committee (TSC)." and lists nine names, one of them "Matt Apperson, OpenRouter". The same person wrote the staff reply in VT8.
- Reasoning items and encrypted content, specification text: "`encrypted_content` (protected reasoning content): Providers MAY choose to never expose raw reasoning to the client. Instead, they can return an `encrypted_content` payload whose format and cryptographic properties are provider-specific. Clients are expected to treat this field as opaque and non-inspectable; it is intended for providers to protect reasoning content while allowing it to be round-trippable." And "All three fields are optional (can be null or the zero-value), and providers are free to support any combination of them".
- Schema, `schema/items.tsp` lines 15 to 30: `model ReasoningItemParam` has `id?: string | null;`, `type: "reasoning" = "reasoning";`, `summary: ReasoningSummaryContentParam[];`, `content?: null;`, `encrypted_content?: string | null;`. `schema/configuration.tsp` line 9 describes the include value `reasoning.encrypted_content` as "includes encrypted reasoning content so that it may be rehydrated on a subsequent request." The effort enum at lines 68 to 74 is `none`, `low`, `medium`, `high`, `xhigh`.
- Extension rule, specification text: "Model providers may emit their own types of items not contained in the Open Responses spec.
